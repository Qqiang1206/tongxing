Detailed Description

The AC production line solution is TXAM's full-line automation solution for manufacturers of complete AC indoor and outdoor units.

Solution Components

The full line covers the entire process including heat exchanger assembly, piping connection, vacuum evacuation, refrigerant charging, helium leak detection, electrical wiring, performance testing, and automatic packaging.

Technical Advantages

  • Precision heat exchanger assembly to ensure heat exchange efficiency
  • Automated piping systems to significantly reduce leak rates
  • High-precision charging systems for accurate refrigerant charge amounts
  • Helium mass spectrometry leak detection with high sealing sensitivity
  • Fast multi-model changeover with short setup times
  • High level of full-line automation for increased production efficiency

Services

TXAM provides full-process support from solution design, equipment manufacturing, installation and commissioning to after-sales service.
