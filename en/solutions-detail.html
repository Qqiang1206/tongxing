Solution Overview

Turnkey automation solutions for TV, commercial display, education conference all-in-one and digital signage manufacturers, covering the full process of dispensing, laminating, flipping, inspection, bagging and palletizing.

Industry Pain Points

Difficult large-size assembly

Manual assembly of ultra-large displays above 65 inches is difficult, with low efficiency and high breakage rates

Complex multi-model switching

Long changeover times and high commissioning costs when producing multiple models make flexible production difficult

High manual flipping risk

Flipping large-size products relies on manual labor, posing safety hazards and potential product damage

Insufficient dispensing precision

Traditional dispensing equipment has low precision and many bubbles, failing to guarantee sealing performance

Low packaging efficiency

Tedious end-of-line packaging processes with high manual bagging and palletizing costs and slow cycle times

Severe information silos

No data exchange between equipment, making digital management and quality traceability difficult

Detailed Description

The TV/Commercial Display Flexible Assembly Line is a turnkey automation solution provided by TXAM for TV, commercial display, education conference all-in-one and digital signage manufacturers.

Solution Composition

The line covers the full process of dispensing, bracket placement, laminating, flipping inspection, and final bagging and palletizing, achieving fully automated production from raw materials to finished products.

Technical Advantages

  • One-click compatibility with 65~110" multi-size products, solving ultra-large-size production challenges
  • Full-process robotic automation, significantly improving production efficiency
  • CAD import for one-click changeover, short commissioning time and minimal capacity loss
  • High line yield rate and good product consistency
  • Self-developed WMS/WCS systems, breaking down information silos
  • True turnkey project with one-stop service

Service Content

TXAM provides full-process support from solution design, equipment manufacturing, installation and commissioning to after-sales service, delivering a true turnkey project.
