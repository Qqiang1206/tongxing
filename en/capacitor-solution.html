Solution Features

Full-line automation solutions for capacitor manufacturers, covering winding, assembly, sealing, aging, and final inspection and packaging.

Industry Pain Points

High winding accuracy requirements

Capacitor winding accuracy requirements are extremely high, making manual operation difficult to guarantee

Complex assembly processes

Capacitor assembly involves multiple precision steps.

Sealing performance is hard to control

Sealing process requirements are strict, and manual operation consistency is poor

Cumbersome aging tests

Aging testing takes a long time, and manual operation efficiency is low

Appearance inspection is challenging

Micro-defects are difficult to detect with the naked eye.

Strict packaging requirements

Capacitors have strict requirements for moisture-proof packaging, etc.

Detailed Description

The capacitor production line solution is TXAM's full-line automation solution for capacitor manufacturers.

Solution Composition

The full line covers the entire process including winding, assembly, impregnation, sealing, aging testing, visual inspection, performance testing, and automatic packaging.

  • High-speed precision winding, achieving industry-leading winding accuracy
  • Automated assembly, reducing manual intervention and improving consistency
  • Vacuum impregnation system, ensuring stable insulation performance

  • Vision inspection system, with high defect recognition accuracy
  • Moisture-proof packaging system, ensuring product shelf life
