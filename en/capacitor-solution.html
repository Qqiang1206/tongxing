Solution Features

Complete line automation solutions for capacitor manufacturers, covering winding, assembly, sealing, aging and final inspection packaging. Our turnkey solutions ensure high precision, consistency, and quality throughout the production process.

Industry Pain Points

High Winding Accuracy Requirements

Capacitor winding requires very high precision to ensure consistent electrical performance and reliability

Complex Assembly Process

Capacitor assembly includes multiple precision operations requiring careful coordination and control

Sealing Control Difficulty

Capacitors have strict sealing requirements to prevent moisture ingress and ensure long-term reliability

Tedious Aging Test

Aging test takes a lot of time and manual monitoring, affecting production efficiency

Visual Inspection Difficulty

Small defects are hard to detect visually, requiring automated inspection systems for quality control

Strict Packaging Requirements

Capacitors require strict moisture protection during packaging to maintain product quality
