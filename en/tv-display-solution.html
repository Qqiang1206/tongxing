Industry Pain Points

Challenges in Large-Screen Assembly

Manual assembly of ultra-large display devices above 65 inches is difficult, inefficient, and prone to damage

Complex Multi-Model Changeovers

Long changeover times and high commissioning costs for multi-model production, making flexible manufacturing difficult

High Risks in Manual Flipping

Large-size product flipping relies on manual labor, posing safety hazards and risking product damage

Insufficient Dispensing Precision

Traditional dispensing equipment has low precision, excessive bubbles, and cannot guarantee sealing performance

Low Packaging Efficiency

The downstream packaging process is cumbersome, with high labor costs and slow cycle times for manual bagging and palletizing

Severe Information Silos

No information sharing between equipment, making digital management and quality traceability difficult

Detailed Description

The TV/Commercial Display Flexible Assembly Line is TXAM's turnkey automated production line solution for TV, commercial display, education conference all-in-one, and digital signage manufacturers.

Solution Composition

The entire line covers the full process of dispensing, bracket placement, lamination, flip inspection, and final bagging and palletizing, achieving fully automated production from raw materials to finished products.

Technical Advantages

  • One-click compatibility with 65~110 inch multi-size products, solving ultra-large size production challenges
  • Full-process robotic automation, greatly improving production efficiency
  • CAD import for one-click changeover, short commissioning time, minimal capacity loss
  • High line yield rate and consistent product quality
  • Self-developed WMS/WCS system, breaking information silos
  • True turnkey project with one-stop service

Service Content

TXAM provides full-process support from solution design, equipment manufacturing, installation and commissioning to after-sales service, truly delivering a turnkey project.
