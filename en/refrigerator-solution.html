Solution Composition

Complex Assembly Processes

Detailed Description

The refrigerator production line solution is a full-line automation solution provided by TXAM for refrigerator manufacturers.

Solution Components

The full line covers the entire process including component loading, compressor installation, panel assembly, piping connection, electrical wiring, online inspection, vacuum evacuation and refrigerant charging, automatic packaging, and intelligent palletizing.

Technical Advantages

  • High level of full-line automation, reducing reliance on manual labor
  • Flexible switching between multiple models, improving equipment utilization
  • Online inspection system ensures product quality
  • Automated logistics conveying with matched process cycle times
  • Intelligent data collection for lean production
  • Turnkey engineering services with one-stop delivery

Service Content

TXAM provides full-process support from solution design, equipment manufacturing, installation and commissioning to after-sales service.
