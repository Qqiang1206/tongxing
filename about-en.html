Development History

A steady evolution history of intelligent manufacturing.

1998/2007

Technical team prototype born, officially registered "Shenzhen TXAM Industrial Automation Equipment Co., Ltd." in Shenzhen in 2007, embarking on the journey of non-standard automated production line exploration and intelligent manufacturing.

2015

Business footprint fully expanded. With exceptional quality, officially became a core intelligent manufacturing supplier for major home appliance and commercial display leading enterprises including TCL, Skyworth, Konka, and Midea, establishing an industry leadership position.

2023

New energy intelligent equipment R&D team established. With self-developed "Circular Automatic Lifting Machine" and other core equipment, debuted at CIBF International Battery Exhibition, fully entering the new energy and automated warehouse logistics track.

NOW

100,000㎡ Shenzhen-Huizhou dual base pattern fully formed.

Self-developed core technologies including UVW platform calibration and ultra-large screen lifting have won nearly 100 patents. Equipment exported to 15 countries and regions including North America, Europe, and Southeast Asia.
