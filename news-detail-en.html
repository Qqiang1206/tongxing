Providing Exceptional Intelligent Equipment to World-Class Enterprises

2023.05.16 | TXAM

TXAM announces that its two major intelligent manufacturing bases in Shenzhen and Huizhou have been fully completed and put into operation, marking the company's official entry into a new stage of scaled development.

Dual Base Pattern Formed

With the successful topping-out of TXAM's 60,000㎡ intelligent manufacturing base in Huizhou, TXAM has formed a "dual-core" layout with Shenzhen HQ as the core and Huizhou as production expansion. This pattern will provide strong production and delivery guarantee for the company to undertake billion-dollar orders from global leading enterprises.

The Huizhou base adopts industry-leading intelligent production line design, equipped with complete assembly, testing and packaging systems, achieving full-process self-control from parts processing to final delivery.

Digital Management Upgrade

While improving production capacity, TXAM continues to promote digital transformation. Through deploying advanced MES manufacturing execution system and WMS warehouse management system, TXAM has achieved real-time collection, monitoring and analysis of production data, greatly improving production efficiency and product quality stability.

The company stated that it will continue to increase R&D investment, deepen the non-standard automation field, and contribute more to China's intelligent manufacturing.
