Single Machines TX-NS-ALM-01

Full-Auto High-Precision Laminating Machine

Core Specifications

±0.075mm Precision 80s Cycle Constant Pressure Multi-Size Support

Product Features

Designed for glass and display modules. Lamination precision breakthrough to ±0.075mm, with constant pressure control, cycle time as fast as 80 seconds.

Detailed Description

Full-auto high-precision laminating machine is a high-end intelligent equipment independently developed by TXAM, specifically designed for high-precision lamination of glass and display modules.

Working Principle

The equipment uses precision mechanical structure with advanced motion control system to achieve automatic loading, precise positioning, high-precision lamination and pressure holding. Full-process automatic control ensures lamination precision and quality consistency.

Technical Advantages

  • Lamination precision up to ±0.075mm, far exceeding industry standards
  • Constant pressure function ensures firm lamination without bubbles
  • 80-second ultra-fast cycle, greatly improving production efficiency
  • Supports multiple product sizes, high flexibility
  • Equipped with intelligent fault detection system, easy maintenance

Applications

Widely used in glass lamination processes for TV, educational displays, commercial display panels and other electronic products.
