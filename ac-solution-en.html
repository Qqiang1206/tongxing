Detailed Description

Air Conditioner Production Line Solutions are turnkey automation solutions provided by TXAM for air conditioner manufacturers, achieving high cycle and high quality mass production.

Full line covers heat exchanger assembly, piping connection, vacuuming, refrigerant charging, helium testing, wiring, performance testing and automated packaging, achieving full automation from components to finished products.

  • Full process robot automated operations, greatly improving production efficiency

  • Advanced helium leak detection for strict quality control
  • Standardized wiring system for consistent quality
  • True turnkey engineering, one-stop service
