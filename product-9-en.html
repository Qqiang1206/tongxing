180-Degree Heavy Load Flip Unit

Core Specifications

15-55s Cycle One-Key Operation Strong Load Capacity Multiple Models

Product Features

Specially designed for flipping large or heavy products, achieving 180-degree precise flipping. Solves manual flipping difficulties due to process requirements.

Detailed Description

180-degree heavy load flip unit is a core equipment developed by TXAM for large-size heavy product flipping needs.

Working Principle

Equipment uses precision mechanical structure and servo drive system to achieve one-key 180-degree flipping. Flipping process smooth, positioning precise, effectively solving difficulties and safety hazards of manual flipping.

Technical Advantages

  • Flipping cycle 15-55 seconds, high efficiency
  • One-key operation, simple and convenient
  • Strong load capacity, suitable for TV, educational displays and other large-size products
  • Multiple models TXM-B-105 series (A/B/C) and 205A available

Applications

Widely used in TV, educational displays, commercial displays and other large-size product manufacturing processes requiring flipping operations.
