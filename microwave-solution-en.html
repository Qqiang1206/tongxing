Industry Pain Points

Cavity Assembly Complexity

Microwave cavity is large, manual assembly is inefficient and leads to production bottlenecks

High Door Installation Requirements

Door installation accuracy directly affects microwave operation and user safety

Complex Wiring

Electrical system is complex with many connections, manual wiring is error-prone

Strict Magnetron Installation Requirements

Magnetron installation directly affects product heating performance and lifespan

Many Inspection Points

Multiple inspection items including microwave leak testing, quality control is challenging

Slow Model Switching

Many models require long setup time, reducing production flexibility and efficiency
