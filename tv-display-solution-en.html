Industry Pain Points

Large Size Assembly Difficulty

Ultra-large displays over 65" are difficult to assemble manually, with low efficiency and high breakage rates

Complex Model Switching

Long changeover time when producing multiple models, high debugging costs, difficult to achieve flexible production

High Risk Manual Flipping

Large product flipping depends on manual labor, with safety hazards and product damage risks

Insufficient Dispensing Accuracy

Traditional dispensing equipment has low accuracy, bubbles, and sealing performance cannot be guaranteed

Low Packaging Efficiency

Complex post-line packaging process, high labor costs for manual bagging and palletizing, slow cycle time

Severe Information Isolation

Information is not shared between equipment, making digital management and quality traceability difficult
