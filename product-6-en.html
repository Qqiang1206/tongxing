Single Machines TX-NS-ALPS Series

Auto Light Bracket Placement

Core Specifications

±0.2mm Positioning 1s/Gripper Vibratory Bowl Feeding 10-20pcs/Batch

Product Features

Precisely grips and places diffuser support columns, providing stable support for display device optical components. Precise positioning, high-speed gripping.

Detailed Description

Auto light bracket placement equipment is used for precise placement of diffuser support columns during display product assembly.

Working Principle

Equipment automatically feeds through vibratory bowl, robot precisely grips support columns and places them at specified positions. Positioning precision up to ±0.2mm, gripping speed 1 second/gripper.

Technical Advantages

  • Positioning precision ±0.2mm, ensuring assembly accuracy
  • High-speed gripping, only 1 second per gripper
  • Vibratory bowl automatic feeding, reducing manual intervention
  • Multiple models TX-NS-ALPS-01/02/03, adapting to different sizes and production requirements
